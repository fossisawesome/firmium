- Swipe a track right to add it to the queue, or long-press it for an Add to queue menu. The mini player also has a queue button.
- Playback pauses when headphones are unplugged or a Bluetooth device disconnects.
- A Shuffle all button on Home plays a random sample of your whole library.
- Albums can be sorted by recently added as well as A-Z.
- Your queue and position are saved to the server, and Firmium offers to resume a queue from another device.
