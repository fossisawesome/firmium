- Firmium now runs on Meta Quest headsets (Horizon OS) as a floating window.
- The Music tab is now a grid of cover tiles grouped into Albums, EPs and Singles. Long-press a tile to add it to a playlist.
- Servers that require a client certificate (mTLS) now work. Add a .p12/.pfx identity in the login dialog or Settings > Account.
- The "Resume server queue?" prompt is now a themed in-app popup.
- Lyrics from LRCLIB now load for tracks with featured artists or (Live)/(Remix) titles.
