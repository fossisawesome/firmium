- The Albums (desktop) and Music (Android) tabs are now a grid of cover tiles grouped into Albums, EPs and Singles instead of a long list. On Android, long-press a tile to add it to a playlist.
- Servers that require a client certificate (mTLS) now work. Add a .p12/.pfx identity (and optionally a custom CA) in the login dialog or Settings > Account. The key is kept in the Android Keystore.
- The "Resume server queue?" prompt is now a themed in-app popup instead of the system dialog.
