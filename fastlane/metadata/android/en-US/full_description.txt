Firmium is an OpenSubsonic and Subsonic music player that also plays local files. It is built fully native in Kotlin, so it stays speedy and lightweight.

Playback
* Gapless playback and crossfade with adjustable overlap and curve
* ReplayGain volume normalization
* 10-band graphic and parametric equalizer with saveable profiles
* Pick up where you left off: the play queue syncs across your devices

Discovery
* Synced lyrics with word-by-word highlighting
* Start Radio from any track, album or artist
* Mix by mood: Chill, Mid or High energy, optionally narrowed to a genre
* Shuffle all with one tap
* Similar tracks, even on servers without native support
* Podcasts by RSS, resuming where you stopped

Your library, anywhere
* Connect to your own server, or play music you keep in the Music/Firmium folder with no server at all
* Download tracks or albums for offline listening, in the original or a transcoded format
* Local play history with CSV and JSON export
* ListenBrainz scrobbling

Looks and platforms
* 18+ color themes, custom themes, and an optional Spotify-style layout
* Android Auto with voice search and an A-Z index
* Android TV with big-screen browsing

Your password is kept in the Android Keystore. There are no ads and no tracking.

Firmium is free software under the GPL-3.0. This F-Droid build does not include Wear OS watch sync, which needs Google libraries.
